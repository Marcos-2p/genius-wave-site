What Should You Check Before Buying?

Before purchasing any digital audio program, take time to review the details that affect your decision.

1. Product Format

Check whether the offer includes audio files, streaming access, downloads, or other materials. Confirm how you will access the product after payment.

2. Price and Payment

Review the current price, currency, payment method, and any additional charges shown during checkout. Do not rely on outdated prices.

3. Refund Policy

Read the seller's refund terms carefully. Check the deadline, eligibility requirements, exclusions, and instructions for requesting a refund.

4. Product Claims

Distinguish promotional claims from independently supported evidence. Be cautious of guaranteed outcomes or claims that sound too good to be true.
